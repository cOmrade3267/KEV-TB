## Executive Summary

CISA added 98 vulnerabilities to the catalog from 1 July through 29 September 2026, 12 of them Microsoft, and six are linked to ransomware campaigns: VMware vCenter path traversal (CVE-2026-59310), JetBrains TeamCity deserialization (CVE-2026-63077), a hard-coded password in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center (CVE-2026-20316), SonicWall SMA1000 SSRF and code injection (CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410), and SharePoint Server deserialization (CVE-2026-45659). Of the 14 cloud/CI-CD tags, ten are dev platforms or virtualization management (JFrog Artifactory has four, plus GitLab, Gitea, Kestra, MLflow, vCenter, and TeamCity); VeloCloud, ownCloud, and Cisco Security Cloud Control match only because the name contains "cloud". FIRST returned an EPSS score for 9 of the 98 CVEs, all older identifiers that were just added to KEV, so the 0.8258 score on Ajax.NET Professional (CVE-2021-23758) should not outrank unscored 2026 additions such as the two Citrix NetScaler bugs due 30 September. Memory-corruption rows stay on the default T1190 label, and WSO2 CVE-2026-5430 is tagged T1005 from the word "traversal" even though the description says that bug leads to file upload and remote code execution.

## Limitations

- **ATT&CK mappings are heuristic, not authoritative.** They come only from the CWE and keyword rules in `config/mapping.yaml`. Review them before using the brief in a detection plan.
- **EPSS is a probability estimate, not a guarantee of exploitation.** It estimates the chance of exploitation activity in the next 30 days.
- **Cloud/CI-CD tagging is substring matching.** It keeps non-matching rows, and it can false-positive on names such as VeloCloud or ownCloud.
